# Similarweb Landing Page Opportunity Research

Research a Similarweb-discovered keyword and landing page from **traffic signal → real user job → natural queries → SERP intent → demand dimensions → demand graph → opportunity decision → site architecture**.

Use this skill when you want to answer questions such as:

- Why does this landing page receive traffic?
- Is the keyword a real generic demand or mostly brand/navigation demand?
- What secondary jobs and generic roots exist behind a brand keyword?
- What natural queries, constraints, and adjacent jobs should be researched next?
- Can the opportunity expand into a tool cluster, vertical site, or programmatic SEO structure?
- Which pages should be indexed first, and which combinations should remain tool-only?

The skill is intentionally evidence-heavy but output-compressed: it produces a short **Decision Brief** plus a deduplicated **Research Pack** and **Research Ledger** containing the research materials that actually influenced the conclusion.

## Example

```text
Use Similarweb Landing Page Opportunity Research to analyze:
keyword: 5 letter word finder
landing page: https://example.com/
```

Or:

```text
Continue digging this seed with the Similarweb landing-page method: wordle
```
